Crafted by Engineers Who Dream

Gajendra and Shubham are two passionate engineers from Chhattisgarh who transformed their vision of premium furniture into reality. With deep expertise in design and functionality, they've created Urban Craft—a brand synonymous with quality, innovation, and artisanal excellence.

What started as a passion project has evolved into a commitment to delivering bespoke furniture that combines engineering precision with artistic vision. Every piece tells a story of meticulous craftsmanship and attention to detail.

Design & Concept

We begin with your vision. Our team works closely with you to understand your needs, style preferences, and space requirements. Every design starts as a conversation.
